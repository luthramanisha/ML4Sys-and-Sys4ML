ML4Sys & Sys4ML 2023

A Tutorial Workshop on ML for Systems and Systems for ML
Co-located with the 20th BTW conference (BTW 2023) in Dresden

The first edition of the ML4Sys & Sys4ML workshop was held in 2023 as a tutorial workshop, together with the 20th BTW conference (Datenbanksysteme für Business, Technologie und Web) in Dresden.

The original website of this edition is no longer available. For the current edition, see ML4Sys & Sys4ML 2027.

Organizers

  • Manisha Luthra
  • Andreas Kipf
  • Matthias Böhm

Workshop Summary

Luthra, M.; Kipf, A.; Böhm, M.: A Tutorial Workshop on ML for Systems and Systems for ML. In: BTW 2023. Lecture Notes in Informatics (LNI) – Proceedings, Volume P-331, Gesellschaft für Informatik e.V., 2023.
